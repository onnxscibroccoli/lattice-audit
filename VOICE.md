# Voice — free tier. Not a market-moving machine.

Layer 2 of STRATEGY.md. This is how the thesis is said in public. It is not an order to buy a ticker. It is not a plan to cause a boom. Free-tier posts do not reprice TSMC.

## Ban list

- "This will cause a market boom"
- "Buy TSM/ASML/IONQ"
- Guaranteed CAGR
- Fake screenshots of fills
- Amending SPEC because a post underperformed

## Allowed

- The barbell: campuses stay; lattice is the option
- Power, wafers, optics, materials as constraints
- Process: kill file, A_RESERVE, four factors not fifteen bets
- 2022 duration risk (two years toward flat)

## Free-tier cadence (autonomous drafts, human publish)

Weekly, one artifact, one human click to post.

| Day | Channel | Form | Length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tue | X / Facebook text | one constraint + one rule | ≤ 800 characters |
| Wed | GitHub / gist | same text as AUDIT or journal excerpt | as-is |
| Thu | YouTube / IG short script | 45–60s talk-to-camera script, no charts required | 90–110 words |
| Sun | Optional longer note | Substack/docs if energy exists | ≤ 600 words |

No podcast host fee. Phone voice memo → transcript → the same text. That is the "podcast."

## First four short scripts (reuse, do not invent tickers)

1. "Large data centers are not dying. The question is what physical thing still gets paid if the next gigawatt is a barn or a node. Wafers, light, power."
2. "Fifteen tickers can be one bet. If they all fall together, you did not diversify. You bought a factor."
3. "A red year is not an exit. A foundry that is empty for two quarters is. Write the difference down before you are tired."
4. "Cash sitting next to a thesis is not cowardice. It is the part of the book that can still act when the tape is ugly."

## Automation on free tier

What can be autonomous: draft generation from THESIS.md + this week's EXECUTION-LOG line.
What cannot: posting to Meta/Google without their APIs and an account the operator owns. Human publishes.
Do not build a bot farm. One channel done every week beats five empty accounts.
